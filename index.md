# What is Axio?

Axio is an **Intelligent Platform Engineering Control Plane** that unifies people, processes, tools, and infrastructure into a single platform. 

Instead of relying on multiple disconnected tools for provisioning, governance, automation, security, and cloud operations, Axio brings everything together into a single intelligent control plane.

Whether you're deploying infrastructure, managing Kubernetes clusters, enforcing policies, or optimizing cloud resources, Axio provides a unified experience that helps teams deliver faster with greater confidence.

> **One Intelligent Control Plane. Everything Connected.**

---

# Why Axio?

With Axio, you can:

- **Accelerate Delivery** – Speed up application delivery with intelligent automation and streamlined workflows.
- **Simplify Operations** – Manage infrastructure and Kubernetes through a unified platform experience.
- **Strengthen Governance** – Enforce security, compliance, and policy-driven operations by default.
- **Enable AI-Powered Insights** – Leverage AI to optimize operations, monitoring, and decision-making.
- **Boost Productivity** – Integrate seamlessly with existing DevOps tools while reducing operational costs.

# Why organizations choose Axio

Modern engineering teams often struggle with:

- Too many cloud platforms
- Multiple DevOps and security tools
- Manual operational workflows
- Inconsistent governance
- Limited visibility across environments

Axio solves these challenges by providing one platform to standardize, automate, and govern your entire infrastructure lifecycle.
