# Operations

Modern cloud environments require consistent governance, reliable infrastructure management, and intelligent automation.

The **Operations** section explains how Axio enables platform teams to manage infrastructure, policies, services, and operational workflows from a unified control plane.

---

## What you'll learn

- State Management
- Role-Based Access
- Module Registry
- Provider Registry
- Service Catalog
- Policy & Compliance
- Automation
- Observability

---

## Why Operations Matter

As organizations scale, operating infrastructure becomes increasingly complex.

Axio simplifies day-to-day platform operations by providing:

- Centralized resource management
- Secure access control
- Standardized infrastructure templates
- Automated governance
- Self-service provisioning
- Continuous observability
- AI-assisted operations
